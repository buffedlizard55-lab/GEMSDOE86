## 1. Four Untried Candidate Geological Hypotheses (Ranked by Expected DTI Gain vs Implementation Cost)

Every `training_features.tif` band index below is verified against `src/gems86/io.py::BANDS` and the empirical pixel-level header-audit overrides in `src/gems86/io.py::BAND_PHYSICS_VERIFIED` (`docs/evidence/band-physics-verification-20261024.json`): in particular, **Band 6 (`tc`)** is GeoDAWN Airborne Radiometric Total Count (`r = +0.997140`), while **`(+Band 18 iso_grav_anom_hg, -Band 11 iso_grav_anom_vg)`** is the signed 2-D horizontal isostatic gravity gradient vector $(\partial g_{\text{iso}}/\partial x_{\text{east}},\, \partial g_{\text{iso}}/\partial y_{\text{north}})$.

### Rank 1 (Selected for Spatially-Blocked Holdout Validation) — `H86H-1` (`H86H-PSTD`): Poisson-Susceptibility Tensor Dislocation & Dilatant Laplace-Gravity Hinge Concordance
- **Specific layers:** `rtp` (Band 2, [DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ](https://doi.org/10.5066/P93LGLVQ)), `tmi_hg` (Band 3), `geod_shearrate` (Band 7, [DOI 10.1785/0220220153](https://doi.org/10.1785/0220220153)), `geod_dilaterate` (Band 8), `tmi_vg` (Band 9, vertical magnetic derivative $M_z$), signed horizontal gravity vector `(Band 18, -Band 11)` ([DOI 10.5066/P9Z6SA1Z](https://doi.org/10.5066/P9Z6SA1Z)), `cond_surf` (Band 17), and `det_elev_slope` (Band 19, [DOI 10.5066/P9MQRCBY](https://doi.org/10.5066/P9MQRCBY)).
- **Physical signature & mathematical transform:** By **Poisson's potential-field theorem** (Poisson 1826; Cordell & Taylor 1971), a homogeneous source body with magnetization-to-density contrast ratio $\alpha = \Delta J / (G\,\Delta\rho)$ obeys $M_z \propto g_{zz}$, where $g_{zz} = -(\partial_x\text{Band 18} - \partial_y\text{Band 11})$ is the regularized ($\sigma = 500\text{ m}$) Laplace vertical gravity curvature. Across an unfaulted lithologic contact, $M_z$ tracks $\hat{\alpha}_{\text{flank}}\,g_{zz}$ smoothly; across a permeable geothermal fault juxtaposing basement blocks, ascending $\text{H}_2\text{S}$-rich fluids alter ferromagnetic magnetite ($\text{Fe}_3\text{O}_4$) to paramagnetic pyrite/leucoxene along the fault damage zone without removing the bulk basement density step. We compute the positive magnetite-destruction **Poisson dislocation residual** $R_{\text{Poisson}} = \max(0,\, \hat{\alpha}_{\text{flank}}\,z(g_{zz}) - z(M_z))$ on $|\nabla_h g|$ structure-tensor hinges, modulated by active Coulomb-favorable dilatation/shear strain (Bands 7–8), shallow smectite/brine conductance (Band 17), and low-relief piedmont/intrabasin cover (Band 19).
- **Why it catches uncatalogued faults:** USGS/INGENIOUS Quaternary fault maps preferentially capture subaerial bedrock and range-front scarps. Concealed intrabasin stepovers, accommodation zones, and piedmont relay structures lose surface scarps beneath Holocene alluvial/lacustrine fill (low Band 19) while preserving basement density hinges ($g_{zz}$) and hydrothermal magnetite destruction ($R_{\text{Poisson}} > 0$).
- **Novelty vs repository:** Zero files in `src/`, `scripts/`, `registry/`, or `research/` couple regularized Laplace $g_{zz} = -(\partial_x\text{B18} - \partial_y\text{B11})$ to $M_z$ via Poisson's potential-field breakdown (`H86E-SPBT` tested scalar basement $\times$ gravity steps; `H86E-MMT` tested 1-D magnetic translation lags; `SESSION_20261024_4D79B443` sketched Fourier admittance on `B2` and scalar `B13` without Laplace $g_{zz}$ or hydrothermal magnetite-destruction polarity).
- **Named non-fault mimic:** Reversed-polarity Tertiary ash-flow tuff margins or caldera ring-fracture moats where reverse remanent magnetization produces negative $M_z$ along a density step without Quaternary faulting.
- **Cost & official data obtainability:** Low–medium (`~10–15 min` CPU); **100% competition-native bands** inside SHA-256-pinned `training_features.tif` (`4371c82e...`) — immediately obtainable in this sandbox.

### Rank 2 — `H86H-2` (`H86H-BPTG`): Bi-Harmonic Basement-Flexure Plate Curvature ($\nabla^4 z_b$) & GDR-1391 2-m Thermal-Probe Lateral Gradient Plumes
- **Specific layers:** `depth_to_base_surf` (Band 15), `(Band 18, -Band 11)`, `det_elev` (Band 12), plus official [DOE GDR-1391](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391) `2m_temperature_probe_INGENIOUS_regional_data.zip` (`sha256: 1301f70d...`, `3,800` shallow probes) and `paleo_geothermal_regional.zip` (`sha256: faffcf69...`, `726` sinter/tufa/travertine mounds).
- **Physical signature & transform:** Thin-plate elastic flexure operator $\nabla^4 z_b = \nabla^2(\nabla^2 z_b)$ isolating buried hanging-wall roll-over / footwall-uplift hinge lines, intersected with anisotropic lateral thermal gradients from 2-m temperature probes and paleo-sinter alignments.
- **Why it catches uncatalogued faults:** Blind geothermal upflow ascends along intrabasin flexural fault hinges beneath 100–800 m of unconsolidated fill, generating shallow conductive thermal plumes and relict travertine/sinter aprons with zero surviving Holocene scarp.
- **Novelty vs repository:** `H86D-CTHB` used radially symmetric point-density halos inside a catalogue-supervised GBDT; no lane pairs bi-harmonic plate flexure $\nabla^4 z_b$ with directional 2-m probe lateral thermal gradients.
- **Named non-fault mimic:** Perched lateral warm-groundwater outflow tongues advecting 2–8 km down-gradient in permeable fanglomerate channels away from the true structural conduit.
- **Cost & official data obtainability:** Medium (`~35 min` CPU); GDR-1391 archives are SHA-256-pinned on reachable GitHub mirrors (`research/h86d_extended_manifest.json`), though sparse probe support limits basin-wide standalone recall relative to Rank 1.

### Rank 3 — `H86H-3` (`H86H-RKTU`): Contour-Crossing GeoDAWN Potassium-to-Thorium (`K/eTh`) Adularia-Illite Alteration Ribbons
- **Specific layers:** Official USGS GeoDAWN ternary radiometrics `geodawn_rad_u8.tif` (`sha256: c22420f7...`, [DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ](https://doi.org/10.5066/P93LGLVQ): `K`, `eTh`, `eU`), Band 6 `tc`, `det_elev` (Band 12), `det_elev_slope` (Band 19), and `rtp` (Band 2).
- **Physical signature & transform:** Narrow (`< 400 m`) anisotropic ridges of positive potassic metasomatic residual $K - \hat{\beta}(\text{TC})\,\text{eTh}$ that cross topographic contours ($|\nabla z_{\text{elev}} \times \hat{t}_{\text{ridge}}|$ high) after subtracting upslope fan-provenance means.
- **Why it catches uncatalogued faults:** Silicified/potassic-altered epithermal-geothermal fault ribs in Tertiary volcanics and thin pediments weather into rounded low ribs lacking sharp Quaternary scarps.
- **Novelty vs repository:** `H86E-MMT` used scalar Band 6 `tc` solely as a quietness veto; `H86D-CTHB` passed raw radiometrics into trees without contour-crossing geometry or upslope provenance subtraction.
- **Named non-fault mimic:** Unfaulted potassic rhyolite dikes, unwelded potassic tuff beds along strike, or evaporitic potash-enriched playa rims.
- **Cost & official data obtainability:** Medium (`~40 min` CPU); `geodawn_rad_u8.tif` is SHA-256-pinned in `scripts/restore_data.py`, ranked 3rd because gamma rays sample only the upper `~30–50 cm` of regolith.

### Rank 4 — `H86H-4` (`H86H-3DMT`): Multi-Depth 3D Magnetotelluric Conductance Root Ratio (`2–10 km` vs `0–2 km`) Sharpened by Analytical Magnetic Tilt Zero-Crossings
- **Specific layers:** Official USGS Great Basin 3D MT electrical conductance slices ([DOI 10.5066/P9TWT2LU](https://doi.org/10.5066/P9TWT2LU)) and INGENIOUS conductive heat-flow grid ([DOI 10.5066/P9BZPVUC](https://doi.org/10.5066/P9BZPVUC)), plus competition `cond_surf` (Band 17), `tmi_hg` (Band 3), `tmi_vg` (Band 9), and `geod_dilaterate` (Band 8).

- **Why it catches uncatalogued faults:** Deep crustal permeable fault roots (`2–8 km`) feeding blind geothermal reservoirs operate regardless of whether Holocene surface rupture broke unconsolidated basin fill.
- **Novelty vs repository:** `training_features.tif` includes only single-layer `cond_surf` (Band 17); zero `GEMSDOE86` lanes ingest multi-depth 3D MT slices.

- **Cost & official data obtainability:** **NOT VIABLE IN THIS SANDBOX YET** — official free public-domain USGS ScienceBase releases exist (`10.5066/P9TWT2LU`, `10.5066/P9BZPVUC`), but sandbox egress is restricted to GitHub/PyPI/npmjs and those binary grids are not yet SHA-pinned in a GitHub mirror. ## 2. Frozen `H86H-1 (PSTD)` Holdout, Budget, Leakage & Gate Protocol

1. **Budget:** Exactly **3** experiment arms (`E1_pstd_full`, `E2_pstd_geopotential_only`, `E3_pstd_inverted_polarity_control`), `< 2` hours wall-clock, `0` weekly submission slots touched.

4. **Gate & Stop Ordering (`AGENTS.md` Rules 4, 6, 10, 11):**

   - Complete v2-bound surface registry check (`scripts/check_h86e_registry.py --surface`) runs **before** full-footprint dot placement; final-dot check runs **before** serialization; any missing/errored registry row, `rho > 0.90`, `PA-86-02` informative firing `> 0.70`, literal full-support failure, or failure to beat both controls with strictly positive paired 95% CI forces `verdict: "negative"` and halts before serialization / slot recommendation.
