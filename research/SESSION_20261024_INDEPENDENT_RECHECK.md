## H33 interpretation and score claims

The repository's pixel-level forensic identity for H33 is a strong account of what changed: the H33 raster is reproduced exactly by removing the mapped-fault near-collar dots from its archived D2.8 parent. Mechanistically, mapped fault cells are masked during evaluation, but only those pixels—not the entire surrounding kernel—are masked. Removing near-collar predictions can therefore reduce false-positive mass while still leaving some distance-kernel coverage of hidden faults. This is plausible, not a causal proof: the collar can also recover withheld truth, spacing near the kernel radius does not make kernel footprints disjoint, and the historical score is not an organizer receipt in this repository.
