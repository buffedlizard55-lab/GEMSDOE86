## Why the user-reported H33 result may be high — and what is not established

The supplied H33 value `0.2778` is **OWNER-REPORTED**, not copied from an organizer submission-page receipt; it is not `ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED`. The value `0.2708` for its parent is likewise OWNER-REPORTED. The local pixel-level forensics in [`docs/evidence/h86f-h33-forensics.json`](../docs/evidence/h86f-h33-forensics.json) measure H33 as the 37,654-dot subset of a 40,199-dot GEMSDOE28 parent: 2,545 dots removed, zero added, with removed pixels at catalogue distances √2 or 2 px. That proves a specific raster edit, not why a private competition score changed.

A metric-based explanation is plausible but conditional. With the shared evaluator, the triangular kernel assigns maximum—not additive—truth credit at each truth pixel. Redundant predictions can add false-positive mass without increasing that maximum. Given the asymmetric false-positive / false-negative weights (0.2 / 0.8), removing redundant or low-credit dots can improve DTI. However, a dot near a mapped catalogue fault is not guaranteed to have zero credit against a different hidden fault, and dot spacing near a kernel radius does not make kernels disjoint. Therefore the observed edit is consistent with more efficient prediction mass, but no causal score attribution or leaderboard projection follows from it.

The repository contains prior holdouts under differing masks, buffers and splits. Their maxima must not be called a comparable current best. The literal witness gate also presently prevents a new valid submission, independently of model quality.

- No single assigned method paragraph appears in the current request; selecting one from parallel sessions would violate the lane protocol.
- The actual template cached feature stack and its receipt remain absent. Native TIFF presence is not equivalent.
- Two indexed registry rasters are grid-incompatible and are retained, not silently warped or dropped; a complete current registry clearance is unavailable.
- The source code cannot diagnose the earlier portal range error without the exact rejected bytes. Current local audit proves only local TIFF/ZIP format/range/grid properties.
- No DrivenData account receipt or authenticated quota is available here. Owner-submitted scores in prompt archives are not organizer-confirmed and no automated DrivenData scraping was used.
- A fault-propensity raster is not confirmation of a geothermal vent, heat source, fluid supply, permeability, or economic resource.

**Handoff order:** resolve the literal-rule/registry witness contradiction through an explicit protocol-owner decision (not a private threshold change); provide the method lane and actual shared stack; resolve the two incompatible registry entries; then freeze matched buffered whole-segment folds, run feature-alone leakage canaries, and only run the best preregistered idea if it beats the strongest same-split control with a positive paired spatial-block CI.
