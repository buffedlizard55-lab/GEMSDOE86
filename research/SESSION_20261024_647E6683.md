## H33 interpretation and score limitations

The hash-checked owner audit describes H33 as deleting dots within two pixels of the catalogue from its parent. This can reduce prediction mass without necessarily sacrificing equivalent hidden-truth credit under an asymmetric distance metric. It does **not** prove that every collar dot is useless, that sparse predictions always win, or that pruning caused the reported outcome. Existing synthetic metric tests explicitly cover these counterexamples.

The owner's reported H33 value, and conflicting current-best values in the request, have no organizer receipt here and are **UNVERIFIED OWNER CLAIMS**, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED scores. We cannot promise to beat them. The owner audit itself contains projection fields and an UNSCORED note, so it cannot authenticate a competition result.
