   - **Max-over-kernel truth credit (`T`):** Each truth pixel $g \in G$ receives $c_g(P) = \max_{x:\,\|x-g\|_2 \le 3} P(x)\,K(\|x-g\|_2)$, so $T(P) = \sum_{g \in G} c_g(P)$ is **submodular and saturating** — a second dot within `2.8 px` of an already-covered truth pixel adds near-zero marginal truth credit $\Delta T$, whereas continuous halos (`P ∈ (0, 0.4)`) cap $\max P \cdot K$ well below `1.0`!

     $$\mathrm{DTI}(P) = \frac{T}{0.2\,T + 0.2\,F + 0.8\,N + \varepsilon} = \frac{5\,T}{T + F + 4\,N + 5\,\varepsilon}.$$
   - **Exact collar-pruning inequality:** Deleting a subset of dots $\Delta P$ that loses $a = -\Delta T \ge 0$ units of truth credit while removing $b = -\Delta F > 0$ units of false-positive mass strictly increases $\mathrm{DTI}$ if and only if
