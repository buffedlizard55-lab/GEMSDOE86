## 2. Preregistered Experimental Arms for `H86I-RKSC`

1. **`E1_rksc_full` (Primary Candidate)**:
   - Combines Lahontan-deconfounded piedmont scarp profile-curvature dipole (`scarp_curvature_deconf`), strike-concordant GeoDAWN radiometric K/Th/U/TC provenance-metasomatism step (`rad_kth_step_concordant`), and seismic-geodetic-argillic conduit favorability (`seismic_upflow_gradient`, `seismic_strain_conductance_gate`).
   - Emitted via shared `vendor/gems57/emit.py::emit_decimated` (`tile_px=3`, `44,090` dots on full footprint, `> 3.0 px` Euclidean collar around visible faults on holdout and full catalogue on submission grid).
2. **`E2_rksc_rad_scarp_only` (Radiometric-Geomorphic Ablation)**:
   - Drops the seismic/geodetic/conductance factors (`Bands 4, 7, 8, 10, 16, 17`) to isolate the pure radiometric provenance/metasomatism + Lahontan-deconfounded piedmont scarp curvature coupling.
3. **`E3_rksc_lahontan_uncorrected_control` (Negative Control)**:
   - Removes Lake Lahontan shoreline contour deconfounding and inverts the potassium metasomatic enrichment sign ($-R_{K/\text{Th}}$) to verify whether shoreline suppression and potassic enrichment polarity provide positive holdout lift.
