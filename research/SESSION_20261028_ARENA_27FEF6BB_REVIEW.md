## GEMSDOE32 H33: plausible mechanism, no causal attribution

`0.2778` is an **OWNER-REPORTED** claim, not a local holdout result or organizer-confirmed score. Pixel forensics describe H33 as a `37,654`-dot field nested in the GEMSDOE28 H27-4 lineage: the parent is reported at `0.2708` with `40,199` dots; H33 removes `2,545` dots in the `1 < d_cat <= 2` pixel collar, retaining its far-field component. The outside-footprint encodings also differ (NaN versus zero). The arithmetic difference between the two owner claims is `+0.0070`; this is **not** a measured causal effect.

The prescribed distance-weighted Tversky index gives truth pixels max-pooled credit from nearby predictions under a triangular 300 m kernel: `TP_w = sum_g max_x p(x) k(d(x,g))`; `FP_w = sum_x p(x) [1 - max_g k(d(x,g))]`; `FN_w = |G| - TP_w`; `DTI = TP_w / (TP_w + 0.2 FP_w + 0.8 FN_w)`. The FP coefficient is `alpha=0.2`, the FN coefficient is `beta=0.8`. Removing nearby catalogue-collar predictions that receive little hidden-truth credit could reduce weighted false-positive mass, but the same dots could cover withheld faults and pruning could reduce `TP_w`. The two submissions are not a preregistered, same-split intervention; their feature/serialization details and organizer evidence do not support a causal attribution.

The `zeros` suffix describes a finite outside-footprint fill and may address a **local file-format** concern. It does not explain a score gain: values outside the scoring footprint are not evidence of prediction quality. The prior `[0,1]` portal rejection's file was not supplied, so its cause is unknown. Do not infer that H33's fill caused the owner-reported result or that a local validator proves portal acceptance.
