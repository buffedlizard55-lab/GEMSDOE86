## Why H33 could perform well — and what is not established

The archived pixel forensics (`docs/evidence/h86f-h33-forensics.json`, not rerun here)
show H33 is its parent minus 2,545 near-catalogue dots, with no additions. Its 37,654
dots have minimum spacing sqrt(8) pixels. This is evidence of a placement/collar
change, not discovery of a new geological layer. Under triangular distance credit,
removing redundant low-credit mass can improve precision without losing much recall.
However, kernel supports still overlap at that spacing, and a near-catalogue dot
can cover a withheld fault: pruning is not universally beneficial. Different sites'
results do not isolate this mechanism causally.

**OWNER-REPORTED, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED:** H33 0.2778 and the conflicting leaderboard
claims 0.3195 / 0.4413 have no submission-page receipt established in this review.
No result here proves a future submission will exceed them. A same-split paired
holdout improvement, independent controls and complete uniqueness checks are needed;
