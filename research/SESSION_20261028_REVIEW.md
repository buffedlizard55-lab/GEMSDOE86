## 3. GEMSDOE32's 0.2778 claim: plausible mechanism, not causal proof

The 0.2778 belongs to **GEMSDOE32 H33**, and is **OWNER-REPORTED** without an organizer receipt. Pixel forensics find H33 has **37,654 dots**, removing **2,545** dots from the GEMSDOE28 H27-4/H19-5 parent field's near-catalogue collar (`1 < d_cat <= 2` pixels) while retaining its far-field component. The parent H27-4 claim is **0.2708 OWNER-REPORTED**. These measurements are compatible with a pruning explanation: under the asymmetric 300 m triangular DTI kernel (`alpha=0.2`, `beta=0.8`), removing redundant near-catalogue mass could improve the precision/coverage trade-off.

That observation does **not** prove the removed dots had zero hidden-truth credit, that collar removal caused the 0.0070 difference between the two owner claims, or that H33 would reproduce the difference under a controlled rerun. The comparison is not a preregistered causal counterfactual and neither score is organizer-confirmed. H32d is a separate **0.2591 OWNER-REPORTED** entry; it should not be conflated with H33. See the prior [H33 interpretation review](H33_INTERPRETATION_REVIEW.md) and dated pixel evidence.
