Submission format

Official requirements include a single float32 layer, EPSG:32611, 100 m pixels, matching bounds/transform, predictions in [0,1], and null/NaN outside the footprint. A valid-looking filename alone is not validation.

Executive summary: how to submit when a candidate is ready

  1. Use a candidate that passed the pre-registered holdout and uniqueness gates; do not promote from projected or owner-reported scores.
  2. Download only the artifact displayed as validated on this page, then use its exact name and the short note recorded with it.
  3. On DrivenData, open the GEMS competition's submission form, choose the single-band GeoTIFF, enter the note, and submit. Check the receipt and keep it with the file's SHA-256; only that receipt supports an organizer-confirmed score claim.

At present these steps stop before file selection: there is no validated artifact in this repository.
