1. Flagged Irregularities for Human Review (Zero-Hallucination Audit)

Every anomaly encountered during data restoration, registry scanning, and metric evaluation is explicitly flagged below rather than silently papered over:

IR-86-01 · Regional Saturation of 201,260-Dot Prior GEMSDOE77__gems85-h85-edti-collar35-skel-r33:
GEMSDOE77 committed a 201,260-dot skeleton (5.34× the normal 37,654-dot budget) whose 3-pixel halo covers 56.03% of the entire 5.17M-pixel Great Basin footprint and 67.32% of all terrain within 3.5 km of mapped faults (where 99.88% of catalogue faults reside). Empirically, historical submissions created weeks before gems85 — including GEMSDOE28 (2026-10-02, 0.7502), GEMSDOE32 (2026-10-04, 0.7861), and GEMSDOE36 (2026-10-04, 0.7849) — exceed 0.70 forward overlap against it despite having |Spearman| < 0.04 and Jaccard < 0.025. Rather than modifying vendor/gems57/uniqueness.py (DENSE_HALO_FRACTION = 0.70 kept 100% SHA-256 untouched), our Three-Regime Tectonic Allocator solves the scientific tip-emergence & blind-basin coverage problem and passes unmodified uniqueness.py cleanly at 0.6831 ≤ 0.70.
IR-86-02 · sample_submission.tif Interior Zero vs NaN Mask Trap (Inherited IR-82-01):
sample_submission.tif contains 5,167,373 finite pixels (some equal to 0.0) and 7,111,787 outside-footprint NaN pixels. Testing sample > 0 instead of np.isfinite(sample) silently corrupts the valid footprint mask. All gems86 and vendor/gems57/grid.py modules strictly define footprint = np.isfinite(sample_submission).
IR-86-03 · DrivenData Portal "Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]" Rejection Cause:
Although competition prose states outside-footprint pixels are ignored during scoring, uploading rasters with outside-footprint NaN or negative GDAL nodata sentinels (-9999) triggers a pre-scoring range error. Our promoted artifact sets all outside-footprint pixels to exact 0.0f with nodata=None (verified n_nan = 0, n_infinite = 0, min = 0.0, max = 1.0).
IR-86-04 · Legacy Character Encoding & Binary Struct Offsets in External INGENIOUS Tables:
Paleo_geothermal_final.csv contains non-UTF-8 en-dash bytes (0x96) requiring explicit encoding="latin1", and 2m_temperature_probe_n83geo.dbf requires exact dBASE III header unpacking <4xIHH20x to parse all 3,800 shallow temperature probe records accurately. Both are regression-tested in src/gems86/features.py.
IR-86-05 · Empty Sibling Scaffolds (GEMSDOE84, 85, 87–92) & Sandbox Network Whitelist:
At audit time (2026-10-22), sibling repositories GEMSDOE84, GEMSDOE85, and GEMSDOE87–GEMSDOE92 were empty initial scaffolds containing no scored or unscored rasters. Sandbox outbound HTTPS is whitelisted to GitHub/PyPI/npm, blocking live queries to earthquake.usgs.gov (documented as Phase-2 external upgrade H86-5).
