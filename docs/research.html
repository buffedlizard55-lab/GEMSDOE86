Why this source

Depth-resolved MT, not another surface-conductivity transform

The official USGS ScienceBase record describes five depth-banded Great Basin conductance GeoTIFFs derived from a 3-D MT model. The inspected predecessor work used surface conductivity and related geophysical bands, but the named depth-banded rasters did not appear in the searched methods/manifests. A fetched world file reports 1000/−1000 map-unit pixels and a projected-looking origin, while the raster facet declares EPSG:4269; internal GeoTIFF tags must resolve this before use. Novelty remains provisional until a complete registry inventory is checked.
