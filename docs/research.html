Mechanism is not validation

Why geometry matters,
and why it is not enough.

The official distance-weighted Tversky metric uses maximum prediction-times-kernel credit at each truth pixel. More nearby prediction mass need not increase that maximum; it can still add false-positive cost. Sparse placement can therefore outperform redundant mass, but removing the only useful cover of a real fault can reduce the index.

With weighted recovered truth T, weighted false-positive mass F, fixed truth count N and the official coefficients, the index is T / (0.2T + 0.2F + 0.8N + epsilon). If pruning loses credit a and removes false-positive mass b, it helps exactly when T b > a(F + 4N + 5 epsilon), for positive denominators. This identity is tested against the shared metric. It is not a leaderboard projection or evidence of why a particular private result occurred.

A density, magnetization, conductivity or relief contrast is not uniquely a fault. The new magnetic-marker idea instead asks whether pre-existing markers were displaced. Even that can be imitated by unrelated contacts, remanence or survey seams. A basement offset can also be old and buried rather than a present surface trace. The scientific reports on the sources page motivate and limit the hypotheses; none validates our untested detector.

First-principles pixel verification of training_features.tif (band-physics-verification-20261024.json) also exposed two critical header mislabels inside the competition raster: Band 6 (tc) is GeoDAWN Airborne Radiometric Total Count (TC, r = +0.997140, min = +2.9534, zero zero-crossings) rather than magnetic tilt, and (+Band 18, -Band 11) is the signed 2-D horizontal gravity gradient vector (dg/dx_east, dg/dy_north) (r = +0.9482, +0.9381), enabling exact Laplace vertical gravity curvature g_zz = -(d_x B18 - d_y B11) without external gravity grids.

The strongest next step is a frozen, visible-only, buffered pipeline comparison, plus genuinely independent and provenance-audited off-catalogue evidence where available. New proxies cannot silently replace the required evaluator. No fault raster by itself establishes geothermal heat, fluid supply, open permeability or an economic resource.
