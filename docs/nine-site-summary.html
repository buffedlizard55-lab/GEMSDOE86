Why the top file scores highest (measured and owner-reported)

  • Owner-reported effect: 0.2708 → 0.2778 (+0.0070). The dot-count and score relation across the nine sites is correlational (Spearman −0.983, n = 9) and is not evidence of cause (IR-86-20261029-06).
  • Consequence: any further pruning of H33 remains a subset with overlap 1.000 and cannot be unique under the 0.70 rule (IR-86-20261029-03).
