The automated gates (project inspection — no human inspector)

  1. Lane & leakage: one preregistered method; every feature alone on holdout folds (AUC > 0.90 = blocked). All five H86-1 features passed (0.52–0.60).
  2. Hide-and-recover: whole fault components withheld with buffers; catalogue-derived quantities from visible faults only, per fold; visible faults masked pixel-exactly; pooled DTI (α 0.2, β 0.8, 300 m triangular) via the unmodified shared evaluator. Candidate must beat the best comparable control with a paired spatial-block 95% CI excluding 0. H86-1 failed (+0.0006 vs random; −0.0567 vs best incumbent).
  3. Uniqueness: surface Spearman > 0.90 or >70% of dots within 3 px of any registry raster = duplicate, stop. Checked pre-placement (surface) and on final dots vs 14 hash-verified rasters: worst ρ 0.070; worst geological overlap 0.292; literal failure only vs a lattice calibration raster covering 99.87% of allowed cells (degenerate witness, certificated). Registry is the accessible pinned set — completeness is not claimed.
  4. Serialization: unmodified shared writer, all-finite zeros policy; reject (never clip) NaN/Inf/sentinels/out-of-range; single band; values in [0,1]; exact sample CRS/shape/geotransform; re-validate from disk; record SHA256. H86-1 passed all of these — which is why a download exists at all.
  5. Audit: python scripts/audit.py re-derives SHA + validator from the TIF bytes and checks the card's verdict matches its gates. Exit 0 means the card honestly describes the file; it never upgrades a negative verdict.
  6. Selector (separate step): only a candidate passing 1–5 may be promoted, after checking the live remaining weekly quota on the authenticated submission page. Local validity is not organizer acceptance.

About the earlier “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]” error

The rejected file was not available for diagnosis; NaN cells, large nodata sentinels (e.g. −3.4e38 leaks from feature rasters) or unnormalised scores are the usual causes. The shared writer/validator used here reject all of those before writing and re-check the serialized bytes: the H86-1 TIF has min 0.0, max 1.0 and zero non-finite cells over the full grid, so this specific error class is excluded by construction — one more reason its rejection here is scientific, not technical.

Science in one paragraph

H86-1 asked whether straight potential-field/topographic edges corroborated by any 2 of 5 physics locate buried faults missing from the catalogue. Answer, on two independent local instruments: no measurable skill beyond random coverage (catalogue-hidden) and below-random on the SGMC off-catalogue proxy — lithologic contacts, the preregistered mimic, dominate those edges. The family's proven gains (dotted 2.8 px emission; deleting dots within 200 m of mapped faults) were retained and are not the bottleneck; the surface is. Next lanes should attack the surface (debiased PU, native-resolution LiDAR scarplet chains) and be screened jointly on catalogue-hidden and off-catalogue proxy instruments.
