What is checked without intervention?

  • Every published TIFF is decoded and checked by the pinned shared validator and template-format checker.
  • CRS, shape, affine transform, float32 band count, valid footprint, finite values in range, outside-footprint zeros and mapped-fault masking are measured from disk.

  • Names and notes are verified against the project length cap. Stored model evidence is not rewritten or upgraded by a format pass.
  • All final artifacts are bound to the negative registry measurement. Unknown/missing/stale evidence fails closed.

Registry checks must fail closed

Candidate checks reject invalid values rather than thresholding or masking them into validity. Missing or invalid registry files, empty/constant predictions and stale candidate evidence cannot pass. Current reports bind the exact source array, decoded pixels and indexed registry content. A surface check runs before dot placement; final-dot checks precede TIFF generation. Historical unbound reports are archives, not clearance, and must be rerun. The representation-aware diagnostics are not permission to ignore the literal stop or spend a slot.
