How We Eliminated the Portal Error "Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]"

DrivenData's GeoTIFF ingestion validator checks the entire raster array on upload and rejects files containing NaN outside the study polygon, ±Inf, GDAL nodata sentinels (such as -9999 or -3.4e38), or unclipped floating-point overshoot (< 0.0 or > 1.0).
