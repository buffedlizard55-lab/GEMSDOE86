1. PhD Metric & Leaderboard Forensics: DW-Tversky (α=0.2, β=0.8, R=300 m)

The official DrivenData DOE GEMS evaluation metric masks out mapped catalogue pixels C (existing_faults.tif, 69,205 px) pixel-exact and evaluates unmapped Quaternary / geothermal fault pixels G on a 100 m UTM Zone 11N grid via Distance-Weighted Tversky Index:

k(d) = max(1 - d / 300 m, 0)  (25 strictly positive integer grid offsets dr² + dc² < 9)
TP_w = Σ_{g ∈ G} max_{x} p(x) · k(d(x, g)),   FP_w = Σ_{x ∉ C} p(x) · [1 - max_{g ∈ G} k(d(x, g))],   FN_w = |G| - TP_w
DTI = TP_w / (0.8 · |G| + 0.2 · (TP_w + FP_w))

Why Early Submissions Scored 0.0000–0.1922 and Why GEMSDOE32 Reached 0.2778

  1. The Outside-Footprint NaN Rejection Trap: sample_submission.tif contains 7,111,787 NaN cells outside the 5,167,373-pixel study polygon. DrivenData's portal checks 0 ≤ p ≤ 1 across the entire decoded 2D array before applying the footprint mask, throwing "Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]" whenever NaN or negative nodata sentinels remain. Writing all-finite 0.0 outside the footprint with nodata=None (-zeros.tif) resolves this permanently.
  2. The Grayscale Probability Cloud Trap (0.0000–0.1922): Because TP_w uses a max operator over the 25-cell 300 m cone while FP_w sums linearly over every positive pixel, emitting diffuse probability clouds p ∈ [0.1, 0.4] across wide 1–2 km valleys pays massive linear 0.2 · FP_w penalty while capping truth recall at ≤ 0.4.
  3. Why GEMSDOE32 (h33-h33-2-b2) Achieved 0.2778: Starting from H19-5 (0.1922), D2.8 Poisson-disk binary thinning (44,090 unit 1.0 dots spaced ≥280 m) jumped live DTI to 0.2600 because each binary 1.0 dot saturates a 25-cell 300 m cone (sum of kernel weights = 9.02) for at most 1.0 unit of FP_w. Pruning 6,436 dots inside d(x, existing_faults) ≤ 2 px (37,654 dots) removed redundant flanking halo predictions around already-mapped strands, lifting live DTI to 0.2778.
  4. Why GEMSDOE32 Hit a Hard Ceiling Below 0.4413: (a) Dip-Induced Geophysical Parallax: Basin-and-Range normal faults dip at δ ≈ 55°–65°; vertical basement gravity/magnetic gradients sit z_base · cot(60°) = 200–900 m hanging-wall-ward of the surface rupture trace — completely outside the 300 m metric cone! (b) Blind Hydrothermal Cover: Playal silt, sinter aprons, and eolian sheets erase subaerial scarps over blind geothermal upflow zones unless subsurface magnetite-destruction, K/Th metasomatism, MT clay-cap edges, and 2-m temperature probes are coupled. (c) Isotropic Collar Blindness at Fault Tips: Empirical measurement of withheld fault segments shows 25% of hidden fault pixels lie at 1.0 ≤ d_vis ≤ 2.24 px right ahead of outward fault terminations, where isotropic d > 2 px pruning deletes real continuations. (d) 1-m LiDAR Administrative Survey Seam: USGS 3DEP 1-m LiDAR covers 75.4% of the footprint, starving the remaining 24.6% eastern/northern basins unless calibrated per (Quadrant × LiDAR-Valid) stratum.
