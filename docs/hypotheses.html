New proposal 1 · H86G-CSPA · NOT RUN

Local cross-spectral gravity-magnetic admittance breaks

Layers and physical signature
Band 2: rtp; Band 13: iso_grav_anom. Within tapered, overlapping windows, estimate gravity-magnetic cross-spectrum, coherence and scale-dependent admittance. Test a spatially persistent change in the relationship across a corridor against within-window phase-scrambled controls. Define numerical windows, frequency bands and regularization only in a future authorized preregistration, before any holdout evaluation.
Why catalogue-missing / novelty
A buried displacement or juxtaposition could change the joint density/magnetization relationship despite lacking a surface scarp. This is a hypothesis, not proof that any recovered catalogue segment represents an unmapped fault. Carries forward the unrun H86F-CSPA cross-spectral proposal. H86F-GZZD evaluates local gravity curvature dipoles; H86F-PCC evaluates signed local gradient alignment. Neither implements windowed cross-spectral admittance. It is not claimed as a newly invented geological theory.
Named non-fault mimic
Unfaulted lithologic juxtaposition, source-depth changes, remanent volcanic magnetization, and mismatched survey resolution.
Qualitative potential / cost — not a projected score
First qualitative priority: potentially information beyond amplitude edges; substantial risk that both fields trace the same non-fault contacts. Medium; windowed FFTs and resolution-matched controls, CPU feasible in principle.
Falsifier
No positive paired spatial-block HOLDOUT-DTI delta against frozen same-split controls, or no gain over phase-scrambled controls.

New proposal 2 · H86G-MPC · NOT RUN

Magnetic monogenic phase-congruency lineaments

Layers and physical signature
Band 2: rtp; Band 13: iso_grav_anom. Use log-Gabor even and Riesz odd responses to identify cross-scale local phase alignment; require consistent axial orientation and test gravity corroboration. Compare at fixed emission budget with amplitude-only magnetic-edge controls.
Why catalogue-missing / novelty
A weak magnetic marker buried beneath cover might retain phase alignment even when amplitude ranking suppresses it. The relevant observation is hypothesized marker continuity, not catalogue proximity. No monogenic, Riesz, log-Gabor or phase-congruency implementation was found in src/ or scripts/ during this review. Existing MMT shifts marker responses; existing ridge/persistence fields use amplitudes and derivatives rather than normalized phase agreement.
Named non-fault mimic
Lava-flow boundaries, dikes, interpolation ringing and GeoDAWN survey seams.
Qualitative potential / cost — not a projected score
Second qualitative priority; contrast invariance might rescue weak structures, but no fault-specific causal evidence yet. Medium; multi-scale FFT filtering and orientation controls.
Falsifier
Phase normalization does not beat amplitude-only ranking at a matched budget on the identical buffered holdout.

New proposal 3 · H86G-PSC · NOT RUN

Persistent potential-field saddle connections

Layers and physical signature
Band 2: rtp; Band 13: iso_grav_anom; Band 15: depth_to_base_surf. Construct scale-persistent Morse-style saddle-to-extremum connections and test aligned connectors across independently filtered potential fields, with graph-topology randomization controls. Do not interpret gravity-derived basement depth as independent corroborating evidence.
Why catalogue-missing / novelty
A buried connecting structure between anomaly bodies might be missed by peak-only ridge detection and surface scarp mapping. A connector alone does not establish fault displacement. Current CTHB uses local Hessian ridges, truncations, cross-gradients and visible-fault relay mechanics, not persistence-ranked potential-field saddle-connection graphs.
Named non-fault mimic
Intrusive contacts, erosional basement relief and saddle points created by superposed unfaulted sources.
Qualitative potential / cost — not a projected score
Third qualitative priority; structurally different coverage, but weak uniqueness of geological interpretation. Medium-high; robust topology extraction and persistence controls.
Falsifier
Connector topology fails to beat spatially randomized connectors or simple ridges on paired physical-block HOLDOUT-DTI.

New proposal 4 · H86G-CNT · NOT RUN

Conductivity-neck topology conditioned on dilatation

Layers and physical signature
Band 17: cond_surf; Band 7: geod_shearrate; Band 8: geod_dilaterate. Test narrow persistent necks joining conductive regions, conditioned on positive dilatation and compared with a broad-conductivity and strain-only control. No MT depth migration can be inferred from this single conductivity band.
Why catalogue-missing / novelty
A narrow buried fluid pathway could connect broader conductive alteration or sediment regions without exposing a scarp. Conductivity and strain alone cannot identify a geothermal vent. CTHB clay-cap margin gradients and FVMB conductive halos do not explicitly extract persistent neck topology between conductive components. This is not a new depth-resolved MT data product.
Named non-fault mimic
Connected saline playa sediments, clay-rich paleochannels and interpolation bottlenecks.
Qualitative potential / cost — not a projected score
Fourth qualitative priority; weakest specificity because conductivity is not unique to hydrothermal fluids. Medium; multi-threshold topology and width/strain controls.
Falsifier
No paired HOLDOUT-DTI gain over broad-conductivity/strain-only controls or instability to threshold changes.

Priority 1 · H86E-SPBT · NOT_RUN_PROTOCOL_STOP

Signed paired-throw basement–Laplace-gravity concordance under low-relief alluvial cover

Specific layers
competition depth_to_base_surf (Band 15); competition iso_grav_anom (Band 13, USGS DOI 10.5066/P9Z6SA1Z) + signed horizontal gravity vector: +iso_grav_anom_hg (Band 18, +dg/dx_east) and -iso_grav_anom_vg (Band 11, +dg/dy_north); competition det_elev (Band 12, USGS DOI 10.5066/P9MQRCBY) and det_elev_slope (Band 19); competition tmi_hg (Band 3, GeoDAWN DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ)
Physical signature
Test antisymmetric flank profiles across +/-400 m strike-oriented corridors: a positive hanging-wall step in depth_to_base_surf (Band 15) and an antiparallel negative step in iso_grav_anom (Band 13) must share the same downthrown polarity AND coincide with an across-strike dipole zero-crossing of the footprint-masked, Gaussian-regularized (sigma = 400-600 m, avoiding 100 m gravity resampling noise) Laplace vertical gravity curvature g_zz = -(d_x iso_grav_anom_hg (Band 18) - d_y iso_grav_anom_vg (Band 11)) along >=900 m strike continuity, weighted higher where det_elev_slope (Band 19) scarp relief is muted by Holocene basin fill, and tested against a polarity-scrambled control at identical 300 m submodular emission budget.
Why an unmapped fault?
Hypothesis: burial by alluvial or playa sediments may obscure surface scarps where det_elev_slope (Band 19) is muted while leaving concordant basement-depth, gravity-step, and Laplace g_zz dipole signatures across blind intrabasin growth faults. Test enrichment on withheld whole segments before claiming preferential recovery of catalogue-missing faults; depth_to_base_surf (Band 15) and iso_grav_anom (Band 13) share USGS OFR 99-383 gravity-inversion ancestry and must not be treated as independent evidence without provenance checks.
Different from this checkout
The base signed paired-throw idea is not wholly untried: H86E E1–E2 tested an unregularized standalone SPBT-v1 surface and it did not beat its polarity-scrambled or spaced-random controls (research/H86E_SESSION_20261024.md). PR #60 claimed signed paired-throw in prose without ever opening training_features.tif (IR-86-SPT-02). This proposal is a narrower, still-untested extension adding a footprint-masked, regularized Laplace g_zz dipole and low-relief basin gate; report it as an extension, not a wholly new SPBT family. Other executed lanes (GMC, RMRB, WVB, GFCO, KSLB, CTHB) use unsigned cross-gradients/ridges; GEMSDOE89 BH-RTP-HABC uses unsigned basement-hinge curvature and GEMSDOE91 S11-MHTR uses unsigned Marussi Hessian eigenvalues rather than this directional parity test.
Named non-fault mimic
Unfaulted pre-Tertiary erosional paleovalley margins or buried caldera moat walls infilled by low-density tuffaceous basin sediments; gravity-basement inversion artifacts (USGS OFR 99-383).
Expected value / implementation cost
Low-cost extension priority only: the standalone SPBT-v1 baseline was negative against the polarity-scrambled and spaced-random controls in H86E E1–E2. Test only whether the added regularized Laplace g_zz dipole and low-relief gate contribute incremental information under matched controls. No measured or projected DTI gain; historical OWNER-REPORTED scores cannot establish causality. Low-medium: oriented +/-400 m antisymmetric flank filters, footprint-masked Laplace g_zz curvature from Bands 11 & 18, polarity-scrambled control, and existing 300 m submodular decoder.
Data / viability

Priority 2 · H86E-MMT · NOT_RUN_PROTOCOL_STOP

Cross-corridor GeoDAWN magnetic marker-horizon phase-correlation offset & native radiometric TC decoupling

Specific layers
competition rtp (Band 2, GeoDAWN DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ); analytical magnetic tilt angle theta_mag = atan2[tmi_vg (Band 9), tmi_hg (Band 3)] derived from competition Bands 3 and 9; competition tc (Band 6, pixel-verified GeoDAWN Airborne Radiometric Total Count TC, r = +0.997140) and optional mirrored geodawn_rad_u8.tif K/Th/U ratios
Physical signature
Hypothesis: phase-correlate transverse rtp (Band 2) and analytical magnetic tilt theta_mag = atan2[tmi_vg (Band 9), tmi_hg (Band 3)] marker ridges on opposite +/-700 m flanks of label-free candidate corridors. Require consistent nonzero lateral translation across adjacent strike windows and compare with zero-shift and phase-scrambled controls. Do not use tc until authoritative organizer metadata confirms its pixel-verified GeoDAWN Radiometric Total Count TC identity (r = +0.997140; never treat tc (Band 6) as magnetic tilt). Estimate survey-seam directions from acquisition metadata before defining a seam veto, rather than assuming fixed compass azimuths.
Why an unmapped fault?
Accommodation-zone transfer faults and oblique Walker Lane / Humboldt relay structures cut buried Tertiary volcanic sheets across basin floors without building prominent range-front topographic scarps, leaving coherent lateral marker offsets (200-1500 m; USGS PP 1658 ch. 5) where surface mapping is blank.
Different from this checkout
Standalone pointwise H86E-MMT v1 (PR #93, docs/evidence/h86e-mmt-run-card.json) was executed on main and returned a negative HOLDOUT-DTI result (0.0290 [0.0206, 0.0381]), shifting tgrad parallel to the gradient normal rather than along corridor strike (IR-86-MMT-SHIFT-AXIS-20261024) and suffering row-major tie corruption on its binary R0 comparator (IR-86-MMT-RANK01-TIE-20261024). This registry entry tracks only the still-untested extension: two-sided +/-700 m flank-to-flank split-window FFT phase correlation along orthogonal corridor strike (-cos theta, sin theta) with multi-window braid consistency and phase-scrambled controls, distinct from GFCO upward-continuation, GMC cross-gradients, and pointwise analytic-signal ridges.
Named non-fault mimic
Unfaulted intrusive contacts, primary Miocene lava-flow lobe terminations, GeoDAWN E-W (200/400 m) flight-line and N-S (2000/4000 m) tie-line leveling seams, and remanent magnetization reversals.
Expected value / implementation cost
No measured or projected DTI gain: the standalone pointwise normal-lag H86E-MMT v1 predecessor failed holdout and PA-86-02 uniqueness gates, while this orthogonal along-strike split-window FFT phase-correlation extension has not been executed. Medium-high: multi-azimuth split-window FFT phase correlation, E-W/N-S survey-seam suppression, and buffered LOQO validation (~65 min CPU, within 2 hr session cap).
