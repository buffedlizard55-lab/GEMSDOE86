Hypotheses (ranked before implementation)

Five candidates, each with layers, physical transform, why it targets faults missing from the catalogue, and its difference from the fleet's ~100 prior lanes. Only H1 and H2 were executed, inside the protocol budget.

H1 · Gravity–magnetic cross-gradient structural coherence (GMC)

Layers: iso_grav_anom (b13), rtp (b2).
Signature: unit-gradient collinearity |cos θ| and opposed polarity max(0, −cos θ), weighted by the geometric mean gradient magnitude, times a structure-tensor coherence term (Gallardo & Meju 2003 cross-gradient constraint, used as a detector rather than a joint-inversion regulariser).
Why unmapped faults: a normal fault juxtaposes dense, magnetised bedrock against basin fill, so both contrasts reverse across the same plane; the joint constraint fires where either field alone is ambiguous.
Non-fault mimic: lithologic or intrusive contacts carrying both a density and a magnetisation contrast.
Difference: the fleet has used single-field edges, tilt, worms, Euler depth and antislope; it never used the joint cross-gradient.

NEGATIVE standalone AUC 0.50–0.53 against withheld truth; adding the four GMC features changes the pooled DTI by +0.0001 (CIs overlap completely).

H2 · Oriented matched-filter posterior sharpening SCREENING SIGNAL — NOT PROMOTED

Layers: any posterior; in production the 44-feature logistic surface.
Signature: eight zero-mean anisotropic Gaussians (σ∥ = 4 px = 400 m, σ⊥ = 1.2 px), max over orientations, applied before the 3 px-tiled allocation.
Why unmapped faults: the DTI kernel credits linear coverage at 3 px spacing; isolated posterior maxima waste dots. The filter converts mass into along-strike coherent chains, and the emission collar (≥ 300 m from mapped traces, 1 km in the shipped off-catalogue variant) keeps the chains off the catalogue.
Non-fault mimic: roads, trails and survey lines in the 1 m-LiDAR morphological stack.
Difference: the fleet sharpens features; no lane sharpens the posterior under a fixed dot budget.

SCREENING SIGNAL — NOT PROMOTED +0.0439 DTI at a fixed 90,568-dot budget (0.07934 → 0.12324), CIs disjoint.

H4 · Spectral Curie-depth / thermal-gradient proxy NOT RUN

Layers: tmi (b14), rtp (b2). Signature: windowed radial power-spectrum slope → depth-to-centroid per 5 × 5 km tile. Why unmapped faults: a shallow Curie surface implies elevated heat flow and a more fractured crust. Difference: the fleet used 2 m temperature probes and paleo-sinter seals as point labels, never a spectral-depth surface. Cost: high (windowed FFT, survey-altitude confound).

H5 · Seismicity-fabric anisotropy NOT RUN
