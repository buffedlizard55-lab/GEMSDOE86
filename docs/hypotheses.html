First: basement mass-balance residual

Layers and signature: iso_grav_anom and depth_to_base_surf; model the basement-interface gravity response, fit density contrast on training spatial blocks only, then find persistent transverse gradients in observed-minus-predicted gravity.

Missing-fault rationale: a concealed density discontinuity inside a sedimentary basin could remain after broad sediment-thickness gravity is removed, without a mapped scarp.

Non-fault mimic: lateral sediment-density change or basement-model error. Compare against raw gravity/basement gradients and reject edge ringing.

Difference / cost: physical forward-model residual, not gravity–magnetic gradient-angle agreement or radiometric regression. Moderate-to-high cost; units and datum must be verified first.

Second: cross-strike magnetic roughness transition

Layers and signature: rtp and tmi; directional variograms on both sides of label-free magnetic ridges, testing a persistent change in roughness exponent and width rather than edge amplitude alone.

Missing-fault rationale: fractured or altered rock might leave a texture boundary away from existing traces.

Non-fault mimic: volcanic facies and flight-line noise. Require cross-field persistence and reject acquisition-direction periodicity.

Difference / cost: damage-zone texture contrast, rather than generic worms, Euler clusters or contact offsets. Moderate cost.

Third: gravity source-shape consistency

Layers and signature: iso_grav_anom, iso_grav_anom_hg and iso_grav_anom_vg; compare scale-dependent horizontal/vertical derivative ratios to extended step-source and compact-source responses.

Missing-fault rationale: an extended buried basement step may be distinguishable from isolated density bodies even without a catalogue trace.

Non-fault mimic: elongate intrusive contacts or basin-margin facies. Include both source-shape controls and bandwidth sensitivity.

Difference / cost: source-shape likelihood, not generic gradient rank or magnetic concordance. Moderate cost; derivative conventions and units are prerequisites.

Fourth: cross-drainage knickzone synchrony

Layers and signature: det_elev and det_elev_slope; derive drainage profiles, identify aligned slope changes across independent channels and reject constant-elevation contour-parallel chains.

Missing-fault rationale: a concealed active structure could perturb multiple streams along a corridor absent from the public catalogue.

Non-fault mimic: lake shorelines, resistant bedding and roads. Compare straightness, elevation constancy and topographic controls.

Difference / cost: multi-channel profile synchrony, not a scarp vote or posterior line filter. High cost; coarse elevation resolution could make the result negative.
