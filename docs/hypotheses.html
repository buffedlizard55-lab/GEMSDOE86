Priority 1 · H86E-SPBT · NOT_RUN_PROTOCOL_STOP

Signed paired-throw basement–Laplace-gravity concordance under low-relief alluvial cover

Specific layers
competition depth_to_base_surf (Band 15); competition iso_grav_anom (Band 13, USGS DOI 10.5066/P9Z6SA1Z) + signed horizontal gravity vector: +iso_grav_anom_hg (Band 18, +dg/dx_east) and -iso_grav_anom_vg (Band 11, +dg/dy_north); competition det_elev (Band 12, USGS DOI 10.5066/P9MQRCBY) and det_elev_slope (Band 19); competition tmi_hg (Band 3, GeoDAWN DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ)
Physical signature
Test antisymmetric flank profiles across +/-400 m strike-oriented corridors: a positive hanging-wall step in depth_to_base_surf (Band 15) and an antiparallel negative step in iso_grav_anom (Band 13) must share the same downthrown polarity AND coincide with an across-strike dipole zero-crossing of the footprint-masked, Gaussian-regularized (sigma = 400-600 m, avoiding 100 m gravity resampling noise) Laplace vertical gravity curvature g_zz = -(d_x iso_grav_anom_hg (Band 18) - d_y iso_grav_anom_vg (Band 11)) along >=900 m strike continuity, weighted higher where det_elev_slope (Band 19) scarp relief is muted by Holocene basin fill, and tested against a polarity-scrambled control at identical 300 m submodular emission budget.
Why an unmapped fault?
USGS/INGENIOUS Quaternary fault mapping captures subaerial range-front scarps where det_elev_slope steps are sharp, but systematically under-maps intrabasin blind growth faults and buried accommodation steps beneath Holocene playa/alluvial cover where sedimentation erases surface scarps while cumulative dip-slip (>150-800 m) preserves concordant basement-depth, gravity-step, and Laplace g_zz zero-crossing signatures.
Different from this checkout
All executed GEMSDOE86 lanes (GMC, RMRB, WVB, GFCO, KSLB, CTHB) discard cross-flank dip-slip polarity concordance; PR #60 claimed signed paired-throw in prose without ever opening training_features.tif (IR-86-SPT-02); GEMSDOE89 BH-RTP-HABC uses unsigned basement-hinge curvature and GEMSDOE91 S11-MHTR feeds unsigned Marussi Hessian eigenvalues to trees rather than enforcing directional antiparallel throw + Laplace g_zz dipole parity inside low-slope basin interiors.
Named non-fault mimic
Unfaulted pre-Tertiary erosional paleovalley margins or buried caldera moat walls infilled by low-density tuffaceous basin sediments; gravity-basement inversion artifacts (USGS OFR 99-383).
Expected value / implementation cost
Highest qualitative priority among local-feature candidates: 100% competition-native, directly targets intrabasin blind dip-slip faults >1-5 km off-catalogue (the exact regime separating R0 0.2778 from GEMSDOE81 0.0041) at low CPU cost (~35 min once protocol gate permits). No score projection. Low-medium: oriented +/-400 m antisymmetric flank filters, exact Laplace g_zz curvature from Bands 11 & 18, polarity-scrambled control, and existing 300 m submodular decoder.
Data / viability

Priority 2 · H86E-MMT · NOT_RUN_PROTOCOL_STOP

Cross-corridor GeoDAWN magnetic marker-horizon phase-correlation offset & native radiometric TC decoupling

Specific layers
competition rtp (Band 2, GeoDAWN DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ); analytical magnetic tilt angle theta_mag = atan2[tmi_vg (Band 9), tmi_hg (Band 3)] derived from competition Bands 3 and 9; competition tc (Band 6, pixel-verified GeoDAWN Airborne Radiometric Total Count TC, r = +0.997140) and optional mirrored geodawn_rad_u8.tif K/Th/U ratios
Physical signature
For label-free oblique candidate fault orientations (excluding pure 090 deg E-W flight-line and 180 deg N-S tie-line seam azimuths), phase-correlate transverse rtp (Band 2) and analytical magnetic tilt theta_mag = atan2[tmi_vg (Band 9), tmi_hg (Band 3)] marker ridges on opposite +/-700 m flanks. Require consistent nonzero lateral translation across adjacent strike windows, a weaker zero-shift match, and use competition-native radiometric Total Count tc (Band 6) to reject surficial alluvial-fan gamma-ray wash boundaries lacking subsurface rtp translation.
Why an unmapped fault?
Accommodation-zone transfer faults and oblique Walker Lane / Humboldt relay structures cut buried Tertiary volcanic sheets across basin floors without building prominent range-front topographic scarps, leaving coherent lateral marker offsets (200-1500 m; USGS PP 1658 ch. 5) where surface mapping is blank.
Different from this checkout
GFCO compares TMI (Band 14) with upward-continued TMI; GMC computes cross-gradients between iso_grav_anom (Band 13) and rtp (Band 2); WVB, CTHB, and sibling ANASIG/BH-RTP-HABC lanes measure point-local analytic-signal or tilt ridges rather than two-sided flank-to-flank lateral phase translation lag; moreover, this formulation corrects IR-86-BAND6-TC-MISLABEL-20261024 by computing true magnetic tilt from Bands 9/3 while exploiting Band 6 as competition-native radiometric TC.
Named non-fault mimic
Unfaulted intrusive contacts, primary Miocene lava-flow lobe terminations, GeoDAWN E-W (200/400 m) flight-line and N-S (2000/4000 m) tie-line leveling seams, and remanent magnetization reversals.
Expected value / implementation cost
Second qualitative priority: physically orthogonal strike-slip / transfer-zone detector using 100% competition-native bands (plus optional mirrored GeoDAWN K/Th/U) without external downloads. No score projection. Medium-high: multi-azimuth split-window FFT phase correlation, E-W/N-S survey-seam suppression, and buffered LOQO validation (~65 min CPU, within 2 hr session cap).
Data / viability

Hydrothermal magnetite-destruction trough & pairwise paleo-vent/sinter strike-alignment corridor

Specific layers

Physical signature
Extract strike-continuous negative magnetic troughs (positive maximum cross-strike second derivative of -rtp (Band 2) and -tmi_vg (Band 9) with positive volcanic flanking shoulders, diagnostic of hydrothermal magnetite -> pyrite/leucoxene destruction) co-located with shallow smectite-illite conductivity cond_surf (Band 17) and surface radioelement alteration contrast tc (Band 6) along pairwise co-linear geodesic corridors (<=6 km) connecting paleo-sinter/tufa mounds, 2-m temperature anomalies, and Quaternary volcanic vents inside dilatant crust where geod_dilaterate (Band 8) > 0.
Why an unmapped fault?
Approximately three-quarters of Great Basin geothermal systems are blind or lack Holocene range-front scarps; ascending H2S-bearing fluids chemically destroy magnetite and mechanically weaken the fault core into argillic creep/sinter zones along subtle intrabasin stepovers, horse-tail splays, and relay ramps omitted from airphoto Quaternary fault compilations.

Named non-fault mimic

Expected value / implementation cost

Multi-depth 3D MT conductance contrast x conductive heat-flow & analytical magnetic-tilt sharpening

Specific layers

Physical signature
Contrast deep (2-10 km) vs shallow (<2 km) MT electrical conductance gradients to separate subvertical fault-fluid conduits from shallow playa brines, co-aligned with conductive heat-flow highs and competition geod_dilaterate (Band 8) > 0, sharpened onto 100 m corridors along analytical magnetic tilt zero-crossings theta_mag = atan2[tmi_vg (Band 9), tmi_hg (Band 3)] = 0 (replacing the invalid Band 6 zero-crossing operator flagged in IR-86-BAND6-TC-MISLABEL-20261024).
Why an unmapped fault?
Blind hydrothermal fault conduits in the Humboldt structural zone and northern Walker Lane frequently lack Holocene surface scarps and modern catalog seismicity, yet deep saline upflow + smectite-illite clay alteration produce multi-kilometer MT conductance steps inside actively dilating crust.

Watershed-crossing 1-m 3DEP LiDAR micro-scarp curvature persistence

Specific layers
official USGS 3DEP bare-earth 1 m DEM tiles (competition 1m_DEM_links.csv); competition det_elev (Band 12) and det_elev_slope (Band 19)
Physical signature
Measure signed 5-25 m cross-strike profile curvature steps on native 1 m bare-earth LiDAR DEMs that retain strike and downthrown polarity across >=3 independently delineated drainage divides, vetoing candidates aligned with steepest-descent channels, constant-elevation pluvial shorelines, or road embankments.
Why an unmapped fault?
Low-slip (0.3-1.5 m) late Quaternary piedmont and intrabasin scarps are smoothed below noise in 30-100 m DEM products (det_elev Band 12, det_elev_slope Band 19) and missed in pre-LiDAR airphoto mapping, yet persist cleanly across multiple ephemeral fan catchments at 1 m LiDAR resolution.
Different from this checkout
Zero GEMSDOE86 lanes ingest raw native 1 m 3DEP LiDAR tiles (prior lanes used only pre-quantized 100 m summary bands in lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif without cross-divide polarity persistence).
Named non-fault mimic
Pleistocene Lake Lahontan wave-cut shoreline benches, fluvial terrace risers, and highway/railroad grades.
Expected value / implementation cost
Fifth qualitative priority: high physical specificity on subtle piedmont scarps, ranked fifth due to external tile-mirroring prerequisite (~716 tiles). No score projection. High: offline pre-staging of 100 m per-tile cross-divide curvature summaries on an unrestricted mirror host, followed by <=60 min in-session LOQO evaluation inside the 2-hour session budget.
Data / viability
