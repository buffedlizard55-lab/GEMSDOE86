Physical signature & transform

Why missing from USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue?
USGS/INGENIOUS Quaternary fault traces heavily favor subaerial bedrock and range-front scarps. Concealed intrabasin stepovers, accommodation-zone transfer structures, and piedmont relay faults lose topographic scarps beneath Holocene alluvial/playa veneers (low Band 19) yet retain basement density steps (g_zz hinge) and hydrothermal magnetite destruction along permeable damage zones.
Novelty vs repository

H86E-SPBT tested scalar basement-depth x gravity step products; H86E-MMT tested 1-D magnetic translation lags (Bands 2,3,6,9); SESSION_20261024_4D79B443 sketched Fourier cross-spectral admittance on B2 and scalar B13 without Laplace g_zz, magnetite-destruction Poisson sign parity, or strain/conductance gating.
