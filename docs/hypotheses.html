Priority 1 · H86E-SPBT · NOT_RUN_PROTOCOL_STOP

Signed paired-throw basement-gravity concordance under low-relief alluvial cover

Specific layers
competition depth_to_base_surf (Band 15); competition iso_grav_anom (Band 13); competition det_elev (Band 12, USGS DOI 10.5066/P9MQRCBY); competition mag_horiz_grad (Band 2, GeoDAWN DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ)
Physical signature
Test antisymmetric flank profiles across +/-400 m strike-oriented corridors: a positive hanging-wall step in depth_to_base_surf and a negative step in iso_grav_anom must share the same downthrown polarity along >=900 m strike continuity, weighted higher where det_elev scarp relief is muted by basin fill, and compared against a polarity-scrambled control at identical 300 m submodular emission budget.
Why an unmapped fault?
USGS/INGENIOUS Quaternary fault mapping captures subaerial range-front scarps where det_elev steps are sharp, but systematically under-maps intrabasin blind growth faults and buried accommodation steps beneath Holocene playa/alluvial cover where sedimentation erases surface scarps while cumulative dip-slip preserves concordant basement-depth and gravity steps.
Different from this checkout
All executed GEMSDOE86 lanes (GMC, RMRB, WVB, GFCO, KSLB, CTHB) rely on unsigned gradient magnitudes, tilt zero-crossings, or Blakely curvature ridges and discard cross-flank dip-slip polarity concordance; PR #60 mentioned signed paired-throw in prose without ever loading training_features.tif or implementing it (IR-86-SPT-02).
Named non-fault mimic
Unfaulted pre-Tertiary erosional paleovalley margins or buried caldera moat walls infilled by low-density tuffaceous basin sediments; inversion artifacts.
Expected value / implementation cost
Highest qualitative priority among local-feature candidates: directly targets intrabasin blind dip-slip faults at low CPU cost (~35 min once protocol gate permits). No score projection. Low-medium: oriented +/-400 m antisymmetric flank filters, polarity-scrambled control, and existing 300 m submodular decoder.
Data / viability
NOT_READY: raw competition layers have pinned mirrors, but execution remains blocked by protocol_preflight.require_feasible() under the literal 17GEMSDOE dense-witness rule. No new external product is proposed as acquired.

Priority 2 · H86E-MMT · NOT_RUN_PROTOCOL_STOP

Cross-corridor GeoDAWN magnetic marker-horizon phase-correlation offset

Specific layers
competition rtp_tilt_deriv (Band 14); competition mag_horiz_grad (Band 2); competition ter_rad_k (Band 6)
Physical signature
For label-free candidate line orientations, phase-correlate transverse magnetic marker ridges on opposite +/-700 m flanks. Require a consistent nonzero lateral translation across adjacent strike windows and a weaker zero-shift match, retaining both dextral and sinistral slip senses.
Why an unmapped fault?
Accommodation-zone transfer faults and oblique Walker Lane / Humboldt relay structures cut buried Tertiary volcanic sheets across basin floors without building prominent range-front topographic scarps, leaving coherent lateral marker offsets where surface mapping is blank.
Different from this checkout
GFCO compares TMI with upward-continued TMI; GMC computes cross-gradients between gravity and magnetics; WVB and CTHB use local-wavenumber, tilt-phase, and hydrothermal couplings. None estimate two-sided flank-to-flank lateral phase translation of transverse magnetic markers.
Named non-fault mimic
Unfaulted intrusive contacts, primary Miocene lava-flow lobe terminations, GeoDAWN survey-block flight-line tie seams, and remanent magnetization reversals.
Expected value / implementation cost
Second qualitative priority: physically orthogonal strike-slip / transfer-zone detector using local bands without external downloads. No score projection. Medium-high: multi-azimuth split-window FFT phase correlation, seam controls, and buffered LOQO validation (~65 min CPU).
Data / viability
NOT_READY: blocked prior to feature construction by protocol_preflight.require_feasible() under the pinned dense-witness overlap stop. No new external product is proposed as acquired.

Priority 3 · H86E-MTCS · NOT_RUN_MISSING_DATA_AND_PROTOCOL_STOP

Magnetotelluric crustal conductance boundary x geodetic transtensional dilatancy bridge

Specific layers
external USGS Great Basin MT electrical conductance grids (DOI 10.5066/P9TWT2LU); external INGENIOUS GPS geodetic strain-rate & dilatation archive (DOE GDR 1391 geodetics, DOI 10.15121/1881483); competition rtp_tilt_deriv (Band 14) and heat_flow / 2m temperature compilations (DOI 10.5066/P9BZPVUC)
Physical signature
Co-align steep horizontal log10 electrical conductance gradients at 1-5 km depth with positive horizontal geodetic dilatation (eps_xx + eps_yy > 0) and maximum shear strain rate, sharpened onto 100 m corridors along GeoDAWN RTP magnetic tilt zero-crossings.
Why an unmapped fault?
Blind hydrothermal fault conduits in the Humboldt structural zone and northern Walker Lane frequently lack Holocene surface scarps and modern catalog seismicity, yet hot saline fluids plus smectite-illite clay alteration produce strong subsurface MT conductance contrasts inside actively dilating crust — the core blind geothermal target emphasized by INGENIOUS Phase 2 expert relabeling.
Different from this checkout
No GEMSDOE86 lane ingests either USGS MT electrical conductance (10.5066/P9TWT2LU) or INGENIOUS geodetic strain/dilatation tensors (GDR 1391 geodetics); H86D-CTHB used only gravity, magnetics, radiometrics, SGMC contacts, and shallow geothermometry.
Named non-fault mimic
Closed-basin evaporitic playa brines and stratiform Paleozoic graphitic/sulfidic black shales generating non-tectonic high conductance.
Expected value / implementation cost
Third qualitative priority: strong physical complementarity for blind geothermal conduits, gated behind external archive mirroring and coarse-to-100 m downscaling verification. No score projection. Medium-high: external ScienceBase/GDR archive pinning, EPSG:32611 reprojection, leakage-canary check against Qfaults-derived layers, and buffered holdout testing.
Data / viability
NOT_VIABLE_YET: public CC-BY-4.0 releases exist, but raw MT conductance and geodetic grid bytes are not mirrored or hash-pinned in this workspace, and protocol_preflight.require_feasible() also blocks execution. Public metadata and CC-BY-4.0 archive listings on https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391 and https://gbcge.org/current-projects/ingenious/ were verified on 2026-10-24, but direct binary download from gdr.openei.org / sciencebase.gov is blocked inside this sandbox's outbound allowlist (GitHub/PyPI/npm only) until SHA-pinned into a permitted mirror.

Priority 4 · H86E-WCS · NOT_RUN_MISSING_DATA_AND_PROTOCOL_STOP

Watershed-crossing 1-m 3DEP LiDAR micro-scarp curvature persistence

Specific layers
official USGS 3DEP bare-earth 1 m DEM tiles (competition 1m_DEM_links.csv); competition det_elev (Band 12) and surf_rough (Band 10)
Physical signature
Measure signed 5-25 m cross-strike profile curvature steps on native 1 m bare-earth LiDAR DEMs that retain strike and downthrown polarity across independently delineated drainage divides, vetoing candidates aligned with steepest-descent channels, constant-elevation pluvial shorelines, or road embankments.
Why an unmapped fault?
Low-slip (0.3-1.5 m) late Quaternary piedmont and intrabasin scarps are smoothed below noise in 30-100 m DEM products and missed in pre-LiDAR airphoto mapping, yet persist cleanly across multiple ephemeral fan catchments at 1 m LiDAR resolution.
Different from this checkout
Zero GEMSDOE86 lanes ingest native 1 m 3DEP LiDAR tiles; GFCO, WVB, and CTHB operated exclusively on 100 m pre-aggregated morphological layers without sub-pixel scarp dipole extraction or cross-divide persistence.
Named non-fault mimic
Pleistocene Lake Lahontan wave-cut shoreline benches, fluvial terrace risers, and highway/railroad grades.
Expected value / implementation cost
Fourth qualitative priority: high physical specificity on subtle piedmont scarps, ranked fourth due to multi-gigabyte external tile-processing cost. No score projection. High: native 1 m DEM tile streaming, hydrologic catchment conditioning, and 100 m EPSG:32611 summary raster generation (~2-4 hr preprocessing).
Data / viability
NOT_VIABLE_YET: native 1 m DEM tiles and coverage manifest are not present in this checkout, and protocol_preflight.require_feasible() blocks model execution. Official competition rules (https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy26osti/96647.pdf, verified 2026-10-24) confirm 1 m USGS DEM features are part of the official public data package, but neither authenticated DrivenData 1m_DEM_links.csv nor USGS TNM tile hosts are reachable from this sandbox allowlist.
