Priority 1 · H86E-SPBT · NOT_RUN_PROTOCOL_STOP

Signed paired-throw basement-gravity concordance under low-relief alluvial cover

Specific layers
competition depth_to_base_surf (Band 15); competition iso_grav_anom (Band 13, USGS DOI 10.5066/P9Z6SA1Z); competition det_elev (Band 12, USGS DOI 10.5066/P9MQRCBY) and det_elev_slope (Band 19); competition tmi_hg (Band 3, GeoDAWN DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ)
Physical signature
Test antisymmetric flank profiles across +/-400 m strike-oriented corridors: a positive hanging-wall step in depth_to_base_surf (Band 15) and a negative step in iso_grav_anom (Band 13) must share the same downthrown polarity along >=900 m strike continuity, weighted higher where det_elev (Band 12) and det_elev_slope (Band 19) scarp relief is muted by Holocene basin fill, and compared against a polarity-scrambled control at identical 300 m submodular emission budget.
Why an unmapped fault?
USGS/INGENIOUS Quaternary fault mapping captures subaerial range-front scarps where det_elev_slope steps are sharp, but systematically under-maps intrabasin blind growth faults and buried accommodation steps beneath Holocene playa/alluvial cover where sedimentation erases surface scarps while cumulative dip-slip preserves concordant basement-depth and gravity steps.
Different from this checkout
All executed GEMSDOE86 lanes (GMC, RMRB, WVB, GFCO, KSLB, CTHB) rely on unsigned gradient magnitudes, tilt zero-crossings, or Blakely curvature ridges and discard cross-flank dip-slip polarity concordance; PR #60 mentioned signed paired-throw in prose without ever loading training_features.tif or implementing it (IR-86-SPT-02).
Named non-fault mimic
Unfaulted pre-Tertiary erosional paleovalley margins or buried caldera moat walls infilled by low-density tuffaceous basin sediments; gravity-basement inversion artifacts.
Expected value / implementation cost
Highest qualitative priority among local-feature candidates: directly targets intrabasin blind dip-slip faults >1-5 km off-catalogue (the exact regime separating R0 0.2778 from GEMSDOE81 0.0041) at low CPU cost (~35 min once protocol gate permits). No score projection. Low-medium: oriented +/-400 m antisymmetric flank filters, polarity-scrambled control, and existing 300 m submodular decoder.

Specific layers

Physical signature
For label-free oblique candidate fault orientations (excluding pure 090 deg E-W flight-line and 180 deg N-S tie-line seam azimuths), phase-correlate transverse rtp (Band 2) and tc (Band 6) marker ridges on opposite +/-700 m flanks. Require a consistent nonzero lateral translation across adjacent strike windows and a weaker zero-shift match, retaining both dextral and sinistral slip senses.
Why an unmapped fault?
Accommodation-zone transfer faults and oblique Walker Lane / Humboldt relay structures cut buried Tertiary volcanic sheets across basin floors without building prominent range-front topographic scarps, leaving coherent lateral marker offsets where surface mapping is blank.
Different from this checkout

Unfaulted intrusive contacts, primary Miocene lava-flow lobe terminations, GeoDAWN E-W (200/400 m) flight-line and N-S (2000/4000 m) tie-line leveling seams, and remanent magnetization reversals.
Expected value / implementation cost

Hydrothermal magnetite-destruction trough & pairwise paleo-vent/sinter strike-alignment corridor

Specific layers
competition rtp (Band 2), tmi_vg (Band 9), cond_surf (Band 17), geod_dilaterate (Band 8), and geod_shearrate (Band 7); mirrored DOE GDR 1391 paleo_geothermal_regional.zip (726 sinter/tufa deposits, sha256 faffcf69...) and 2m_temperature_probe_INGENIOUS_regional_data.zip (3,800 probes, sha256 1301f70d...); mirrored DOE GDR 1391 gdr_volcanic_vents_in_footprint.csv and gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv (excluding dist_known_fault_px) + geodawn_extensions_u8.tif K/Th & U/Th
Physical signature
Extract strike-continuous negative magnetic troughs (positive maximum cross-strike second derivative of -rtp Band 2 and -tmi_vg Band 9 with positive volcanic flanking shoulders, diagnostic of hydrothermal magnetite -> pyrite/leucoxene destruction) along pairwise co-linear geodesic corridors (<=6 km) connecting paleo-sinter/tufa mounds, 2-m temperature anomalies, and Quaternary volcanic vents inside dilatant conductive crust (cond_surf Band 17 x geod_dilaterate Band 8).
Why an unmapped fault?
Approximately three-quarters of Great Basin geothermal systems are blind or lack Holocene range-front scarps; ascending H2S-bearing fluids chemically destroy magnetite along subtle intrabasin stepovers, horse-tail splays, and relay ramps omitted from airphoto Quaternary fault compilations.

Named non-fault mimic

Expected value / implementation cost

Multi-depth 3D MT conductance contrast x conductive heat-flow & transtensional dilatancy bridge

Specific layers
external USGS Great Basin 3D MT electrical conductance slices (5 depth ranges 2-200 km, DOI 10.5066/P9TWT2LU); external USGS Great Basin conductive heat-flow grids (DOI 10.5066/P9BZPVUC); competition cond_surf (Band 17), geod_dilaterate (Band 8), geod_shearrate (Band 7), and tc (Band 6)
Physical signature
Contrast deep (2-10 km) vs shallow (<2 km) MT electrical conductance gradients to separate subvertical fault-fluid conduits from shallow playa brines, co-aligned with conductive heat-flow highs and competition geod_dilaterate (Band 8) > 0, sharpened onto 100 m corridors along tc (Band 6) magnetic tilt zero-crossings.
Why an unmapped fault?
Blind hydrothermal fault conduits in the Humboldt structural zone and northern Walker Lane frequently lack Holocene surface scarps and modern catalog seismicity, yet deep saline upflow + smectite-illite clay alteration produce multi-kilometer MT conductance steps inside actively dilating crust.

Named non-fault mimic

Expected value / implementation cost

Watershed-crossing 1-m 3DEP LiDAR micro-scarp curvature persistence

Specific layers
official USGS 3DEP bare-earth 1 m DEM tiles (competition 1m_DEM_links.csv); competition det_elev (Band 12) and det_elev_slope (Band 19)
Physical signature
Measure signed 5-25 m cross-strike profile curvature steps on native 1 m bare-earth LiDAR DEMs that retain strike and downthrown polarity across independently delineated drainage divides, vetoing candidates aligned with steepest-descent channels, constant-elevation pluvial shorelines, or road embankments.
Why an unmapped fault?
Low-slip (0.3-1.5 m) late Quaternary piedmont and intrabasin scarps are smoothed below noise in 30-100 m DEM products (det_elev Band 12, det_elev_slope Band 19) and missed in pre-LiDAR airphoto mapping, yet persist cleanly across multiple ephemeral fan catchments at 1 m LiDAR resolution.
Different from this checkout
Zero GEMSDOE86 lanes ingest raw native 1 m 3DEP LiDAR tiles (prior lanes used only pre-quantized 100 m summary bands in lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif without cross-divide polarity persistence).
Named non-fault mimic
Pleistocene Lake Lahontan wave-cut shoreline benches, fluvial terrace risers, and highway/railroad grades.
Expected value / implementation cost
Fifth qualitative priority: high physical specificity on subtle piedmont scarps, ranked fifth due to multi-gigabyte external tile-streaming cost (~716 tiles). No score projection. High: native 1 m DEM tile streaming, hydrologic catchment conditioning, and 100 m EPSG:32611 summary raster generation (~2-4 hr preprocessing).
Data / viability
NOT_VIABLE_YET: native 1 m DEM tiles and coverage manifest are not present in this checkout, and protocol_preflight.require_feasible() blocks model execution.
