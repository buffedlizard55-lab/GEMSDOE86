Physical signature: Track the lateral displacement vector of coherent conductivity-gradient ridges between consecutive depth slices; require monotonic migration consistent with a dipping plane, then project the inferred plane to the surface. Do not use a single shallow/deep gradient magnitude as the score.

Why it may reveal a catalogue-missing fault: A buried dipping conduit or alteration zone can be laterally displaced from its surface trace. Cross-depth migration could therefore prioritize a blind structure not present in a surface-fault catalogue.

Named non-fault mimic: Depth-dependent inversion smoothing or a basin-margin conductor; require coherent displacement across adjacent depth slices and sensitivity to inversion resolution before interpreting it as a fault.

Rank 2 — H86D-PFCO: Paired potential-field ridge dislocation with transverse gravity step

Layers:

  • Competition RTP/TMI, upward-continued TMI, complete Bouguer gravity, and basement-depth bands

Physical signature: Extract phase-symmetry ridge segments at more than one scale; graph-match conjugate terminations with consistent strike across a lateral offset; score only the intervening corridor when a transverse gravity-gradient step corroborates it.

Why it may reveal a catalogue-missing fault: A concealed cross-fault can displace an older magnetic marker contact. Scoring the termination/offset corridor rather than the contact itself may reveal the cross-fault away from mapped surface traces.

Named non-fault mimic: Caldera-ring geometry, intrusive contacts, and survey-mosaic seams; require conjugate geometry across scales and independent gravity support, and test acquisition-axis concentration.

Difference from existing code: The paired-endpoint and transverse-bridge formulation adds multi-scale matching and corroboration to earlier potential-field edge/contact features. A related displaced-contact concept already appears as a prior candidate in research/REVIEW.md, so this is a refinement, not a wholly new repository idea.

Implementation cost: High: ridge graph construction, endpoint pairing, offset constraints, and fold-stable controls.

Data availability / viability: Uses competition geophysics/topography channels only, if the official feature stack is restored. No new external raster is required. The shared cached stack is absent here, so it is not currently viable to validate under the prompt.
