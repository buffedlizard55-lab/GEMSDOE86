Deleting 6,436 redundant dots hugging already-mapped traces shaved ~12,472 weighted FP units from the denominator while sacrificing almost zero new-fault TP_w, producing a mechanical +0.0178 ratio bump (0.2600 → 0.2778) and fixing NaN upload failures.
